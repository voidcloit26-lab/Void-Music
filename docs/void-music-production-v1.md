# VOID Music V1 production plan — isolated candidate

Baseline: 5c045aaff941893ccd0254604d4b3f167f7c6469 (working internal APK).
No live deployment, migration, release publication or signing-key change is authorized by this branch alone.

## Sequence and release gates
1. Permanent account identity: random server ID, passkey (WebAuthn / Android Credential Manager), hashed one-time recovery codes, device key challenge. No IMEI, phone tracking or hidden file. Android ID only optional recognition, not authentication. Existing active installations claim an account using verified legacy key possession; revoked accounts cannot migrate. Lost legacy keys require owner-assisted recovery, never automatic entitlement transfer based on a numeric ID alone.
2. Cloud metadata: account-scoped playlists, favorites, saved albums/artists, preferences, optional history, revision/conflict handling and explicit export/delete. Do not upload audio or private local-file paths. Keep recovery credentials separate from backups. Test reinstall and offline reconnection.
3. Update center: signed stable policy, separate release signing key, immutable versioned APK, authenticated download, verified SHA-256 and expected APK signer/package/version, Android installer confirmation. Stable minimum defaults to latest; test channel isolated. Persist last verified policy, reject replay, restrict online access for obsolete supported clients. No unverified outage can invent a required update. Keep update/recovery UI reachable; preserve data. Offline lease needs explicit design, testing, and migration from lifetime receipts. Older legacy offline APKs cannot be retroactively forced to update.
4. Owner controls: account/device replacement, audit logs, recovery and revocation, publish/promote release, version stats with privacy controls. Emergency policy rollback uses a higher policy sequence; APK recovery uses a fixed build with a higher versionCode.
5. UI: keep working player intact; fix misleading native connection label and preview overlap, use glass preview only in app, Spotify search default when exact link unavailable, keep MARS button. Discord controls actual activity rendering.
6. Branding and legal: translate visible legacy strings, audit icons/links, keep accessible About legal notices and GPL corresponding source, review Discord SDK and music provider permissions before distribution.
7. Production signing: permanent key with secure offline backup, stable package online.voidbot.music, monotonic versionCode, R8 and resource shrinking, release verification, no test key. Existing .fast test app cannot directly update to a different package/signing identity; migrate accounts and library first. Never commit private signing or server keys.
8. Validate isolated PostgreSQL, Android compilation, migration, recovery, authorization, updater tampering and outage cases; full phone QA; only then promote approved release.

Initial implementation: guardian/release_policy.py and tests, not connected to live API or Android.
Keep VOID Bot, Lavalink and MARS services/databases unchanged.

## Approved membership business design (2026-11-26)
Planning only; no paid launch or schema/API change authorized by this document alone.
- Annual Premium USD-denominated price (proposed USD 5 per 12 calendar months, configurable and not yet approved as final), manually confirmed crypto payment, no integrated payment gateway or automatic renewal. Start at successful redemption. Early renewal extends from current expiry; expired renewal starts at redemption. Define UTC and leap-day handling explicitly.
- Complimentary lifetime invitations: owner-only generation, one redemption, one active device slot, no scheduled expiry while service operates, no charge. Existing lifetime entitlements remain grandfathered. Trials and complimentary fixed-term access separately tracked. Account identity independent of membership. Suspended/revoked distinct from expired; renewal never overrides a security suspension. Preserve library on expiry; authenticated export/deletion remain available.
- Store keyed hashes of high-entropy codes; separate invitation expiry from membership expiry; atomic redemption, account-bound renewal, replay protection, audit trail, owner reauthentication for sensitive changes, idempotent manual payment records (no banking secrets), refunds/voiding ledger. Distinguish paid and free members in revenue metrics. Owner-assisted device recovery, no hardware tracking.
- Server-authoritative expiration; bounded signed offline leases capped at paid expiry, explicit connectivity and clock rollback tests, no permanent offline bypass. Account recovery before signing/package migration. Notices at 30/7/1 days and expiry, opt-in communications; no automatic charges.
- Before collecting payment disclose annual price, services included, material third-party dependencies, no guaranteed track catalog or continuous integrations, renewal and expiry, refunds and shutdown policy. Terms acceptance version/time recorded; separate optional data consent. Accessible Terms, Privacy, Refunds, Licenses and support before payment/activation and in About. No blanket no-liability/no-refund disclaimer or waiver of statutory consumer rights. Reasonable remedies for material loss of paid services, e.g. extension or proportionate unused-term refund as appropriate, with legal rights preserved. Stop sales on known major outage, notify and provide export window on shutdown where feasible.
- Privacy: data inventory, purpose/minimization, retention schedule and deletion/export, security, processor disclosure, breach response, children/consent compliance and complaint contact. Father/adult legal operator to confirm business details, tax, contracts, consumer and privacy obligations. No claim of affiliation with music providers or Discord; preserve GPL rights and accessible notices/corresponding source; review third-party SDK and music permissions before commercial launch. Do not charge for unauthorized third-party content access.
- Release gate: adult operator and qualified Indian legal review of actual Terms/Privacy/Refund policy and licensing; verified membership expiry/renewal/recovery/security tests, backups, and permanent signing. No paid customers until gates pass.

## USD and manual crypto payment revision
No wallet, chain custody, exchange integration or automatic payment processing in the app. Adult operator approves allowed networks, settlement evidence, tax/accounting and compliance before sales. Store quoted USD integer cents, actual asset amount as exact decimal/base units, network and canonical transaction reference, confirmed time, quote valuation and applicable local accounting values. Unique settlement reference and atomic consumption are mandatory before issuing paid membership keys; handle underpayment, overpayment, duplicate and refund cases. Do not store wallet secrets. Paid launch disabled until licensing and legal gates pass. Initial membership_rules.py implements pure transitions only; persistent transaction store, account authentication, API, Android and production migration remain pending.

## Clerk authentication evaluation
Recommended: Clerk native Android SDK for sign-in (start with verified email codes; Google optional), Guardian remains the authority for membership, device proof, owner permissions and updates; Neon stores stable internal account IDs and cloud metadata. Bind an external subject to the configured issuer, never to mutable email or client-provided identity fields. Existing accounts link only after verified legacy/current device proof plus verified Clerk session. Do not silently migrate lost devices or grant access on sign-up. Recovery requires a verified linked identity plus recovery proof or reviewed owner-assisted device replacement. Clerk Hobby plan lacks passkeys, MFA, and built-in minimum app version enforcement as of 2026-11-26; keep owner authentication independent and use Guardian updater. No paid plan selected. Before integration, verify session signatures with a maintained JWT library/SDK, pinned issuer and expected audience/authorized parties as configured, expiry/not-before, session status, bounded key refresh and failure behavior. Never bundle server secret keys. Initial external_identity_store.py is a local-only linking prototype, not a token verifier; no live Clerk tenant or Android SDK configured.
